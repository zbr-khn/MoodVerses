1. Introduction

MoodVerses ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. MoodVerses is designed with an offline-first, privacy-respecting architecture that ensures your spiritual contemplation and Quranic reflections remain personal, secure, and private.

2. Information We Collect

MoodVerses operates with minimal data collection:

  • Guest Usage (Offline Mode): When using MoodVerses without an account, zero personal data is collected or transmitted. All saved favorite Ayat, custom names, and Tasbih counts are stored strictly locally on your device via secure local storage.
  • Account Information (Optional Cloud Sync): If you choose to create an account or sign in using Google Sign-In, Email/Password, or Phone Authentication, we store your account identifier (email address, phone number, and optional display name) securely through Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Cloud Synchronized Favorites and Chats: If logged in, your saved Ayat and Waleey conversation history are synced to Google Cloud Firestore solely to allow you to access your spiritual journey across multiple devices.

3. How We Use Your Information

Any information collected is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate your session and protect your account.
  • To synchronize your saved favorites and conversation sessions across your authorized devices.
  • To personalize your experience (e.g. addressing you by your chosen name during spiritual greetings).

4. Artificial Intelligence & Spiritual Companion Inquiries

When you ask questions to Waleey, queries are processed securely to provide verified Quranic and Sunnah citations. Your queries are never used to train generalized advertising models, are not sold to third parties, and contain no persistent identifying tracking parameters.

5. Third-Party Infrastructure

MoodVerses relies on industry-standard cloud infrastructure provided by Google Cloud and Google Firebase. These services operate under strict confidentiality and security compliance (SOC 1, SOC 2, ISO 27001). We do not use third-party advertising SDKs, data brokers, or marketing trackers.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

You maintain full control over your data:

  • You can clear your local cache, chat sessions, and saved favorites at any time from within the app.
  • You can request complete deletion of your account and associated cloud data by contacting our support team or logging out and resetting your local profile.

7. Children's Privacy

MoodVerses is suitable for users of all ages seeking beneficial Quranic knowledge. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact the MoodVerses team via our official GitHub repository: github.com/zbr-khn/MoodVerses.
